## Title
Your Body Is Glowing Right Now (You Just Can't See It)

## Description
Your body gives off real, visible light, about a thousand times too faint for your eyes to see. In 2009, scientists in Japan photographed it with an ultra-sensitive camera in a sealed dark room: the glow was strongest on the face and changed through the day, dimmest at 10 am and brightest around 4 pm. It comes from ordinary chemistry in your cells, the same kind that makes a glow stick shine. In 2025, a study in Canada found that living mice glowed and the glow almost vanished after they died, and that damaged leaves glowed brighter. One day, this faint light may help doctors check tissue and farmers spot sick crops.

Sources: Kobayashi et al., PLoS ONE (2009); Salari, Oblak et al., The Journal of Physical Chemistry Letters (2025).

## Title

Why This Crew Abandoned a Perfectly Good Ship

## Description

In 1916, a rusty old steamer drifts alone in the North Atlantic, and its crew can't wait to abandon it. Nothing is wrong with the ship. That's the point.

Britain's Q-ships were decoys: harmless-looking cargo ships with guns hidden in fake deckhouses and even hen coops. When a U-boat surfaced to finish them off with its deck gun, a rehearsed "panic party" rowed away while the gun crews lay flat on deck, waiting for the sub to come close.

The scene is a dramatised illustration; the tactic is real.

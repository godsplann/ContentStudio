## Title

The Soldier Who Ate His Cigarettes

## Description

In 1942, Polish soldiers in Iran bought an orphaned bear cub from a boy and named him Wojtek. He grew up drinking their beer and eating their cigarettes. When a British troopship wouldn't take pets, they solved it the army way: Wojtek was enlisted, with a rank, a paybook and a serial number.

At Monte Cassino in 1944, his comrades said he carried crates of artillery shells like the rest of them. He was promoted to corporal, and his company's badge became a bear carrying a shell.

The officer, his line and the desk scenes are a dramatised illustration; Wojtek's story is real.

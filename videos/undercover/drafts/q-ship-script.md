# Undercover draft script: Q-ships (18 November 2026)

Status: draft, facts checked, waiting for the owner's OK and a narrator. About 170 words, about 60 s.
The captain, the young sailor, the parrot cage and the lines of dialogue are an invented illustration; the Q-ship tactics are documented.

## Script

The captain ordered his crew to abandon ship. Then he lay down flat on the deck and didn't move.
You see, it's 1917, and a German submarine has just surfaced beside his scruffy little cargo ship.
So half the crew piles into the lifeboats, shouting and waving, one guy even clutching a parrot cage.
A young sailor whispers, "Sir, shouldn't we be in the boats too?"
"Lie still. You're cargo now."
The submarine creeps closer. Its captain wants to save his torpedoes, so he'll finish this wreck with his deck gun.
Closer. He's reading the ship's name through his binoculars. It's fake. So is the cargo. So is the chicken coop.
So what is this ship?
Well, it's called a Q-ship. A warship dressed up as a helpless freighter, with guns hidden behind hinged walls, fake crates and even chicken coops. The crew rowing away? That's the "panic party". They rehearsed it.
The submarine pulls up close enough to throw a rope.
Up goes the navy flag.
And the walls fall down.

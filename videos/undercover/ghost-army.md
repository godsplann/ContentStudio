## Description

France, 1944. Two Frenchmen on bicycles watched four American soldiers lift a 40-ton Sherman tank. "The Americans are very strong," one soldier told them.

Then things got stranger: tank tracks into empty fields, tank noises all night with no tanks in sight, and a general who wasn't a general. Watch to the end to find out what was really going on.

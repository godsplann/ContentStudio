# Undercover: The Woman Ships Couldn't Sink (script, 19 November 2026)

**Status:** facts checked; waiting for the owner's go-ahead before voicing.
**Working title:** The Woman Ships Couldn't Sink
**Target length:** under 75 s (owner: under a minute, 1:15 at most).

## Script (148 words, about 63 s at Albert's pace)

Opens with a one-line hook (first 2-3 s), then the formula.

She survived the Titanic. It wasn't even her closest call.

Aegean Sea, 1916. A ship is sinking. A nurse runs back to her cabin. For her toothbrush.

Her name is Violet Jessop.

Five years earlier, she works on the Olympic. A warship crashes into it.

Next, she joins its sister ship. The Titanic.

The night it sinks, an officer hands her a baby in a lifeboat.

Violet survives.

But on the rescue ship, she moans about one thing. No toothbrush.

Her friend teases her. "Never start another disaster without your toothbrush."

Now she's on the third sister ship. The Britannic.

It hits a mine. Violet grabs her toothbrush.

Her lifeboat drifts toward the spinning propellers.

Violet jumps. She hits her head under the water.

And she still makes it out.

People called her Miss Unsinkable.

And when the boats pull her from the sea, she checks her pocket.

## Shot notes

- Opening: a tilting hospital-ship corridor; a nurse in Red Cross uniform dashing into a cabin and grabbing a toothbrush off the sink.
- Place-and-year: AEGEAN 1916 stencilled on a lifebelt.
- Flashback beats clearly labelled with the ship's name painted on each hull (OLYMPIC, TITANIC) and a sepia grade, then back to full colour for BRITANNIC.
- Doubter: the friend on the rescue ship, teasing with a toothbrush gesture.
- Plant the toothbrush in the opening, the same framing as the end.
- Keep the sinkings gentle: no bodies, no panic crowds, calm night sea for Titanic.
- Last frame: Violet in a lifeboat, wet hair, hand going to her pocket. Cut.

## Fact check

- Olympic collided with HMS Hawke on 20 September 1911 with Jessop aboard. Sources: Wikipedia "Violet Jessop"; History.co.uk; Business Insider.
- On Titanic (1912) she was ordered into lifeboat 16 and an officer handed her a baby. Sources: Boing Boing; History Hit; History.co.uk.
- After Titanic she complained about having no toothbrush; a friend joked she should never undertake another disaster without one (line shortened from her memoir). Sources: National Geographic; worldhistory.us; Today I Found Out.
- Britannic, a hospital ship, struck a mine on 21 November 1916 in the Aegean; she grabbed her toothbrush. Sources: National Geographic; Mental Floss; Business Insider.
- She jumped from a lifeboat being drawn toward the propellers and suffered a head injury. Sources: Wikipedia; Business Insider (Maxtone-Graham preface).
- Nickname "Miss Unsinkable". Sources: Today I Found Out; divelog.gr.
- Invented for illustration: the pocket check at the end (she did have the toothbrush).

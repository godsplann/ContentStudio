# Undercover: YouTube channel description

Written 18 November 2026. Paste the text below into YouTube Studio > Customisation > Basic info > Description. It also works for the Instagram and Facebook bios (shortened version at the end).

## Full description

Little-known stories. Big twists.

Undercover digs up the strange, true stories hiding under everyday history and science: the decoy ship that wanted to be attacked, the horse that fooled a panel of experts, the clever tricks, odd inventions and happy accidents that never made it into the textbooks.

Every Short starts with something that makes no sense and ends with the twist that explains it, all in about a minute.

Every story is checked against reliable sources before it is made. Where a scene is dramatised to show how something worked, the facts behind it are real.

Subscribe and lift the lid on a new story.
